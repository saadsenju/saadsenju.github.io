JARVIS privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

JARVIS is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

What Google data it uses, and why

  • Gmail: reading, searching, drafting and organising (archive, labels, read state, moving to Trash) the owner's own email, only when the owner asks. Sending, trashing and sharing require the owner's explicit approval each time.
  • Google Drive: finding the owner's files and sharing them with people the owner names, only after the owner approves.

Where the data goes

  • Data is processed on the owner's own Mac. Access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • When the owner asks JARVIS to do a task, the relevant content is sent to the AI model provider the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude) to carry out that task.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
