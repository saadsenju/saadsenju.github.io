Rose text messages: privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Rose is a personal AI assistant run by Saad Paracha (sole proprietor) for his own use. Its text messages go to one person only: the account owner, on the mobile number he entered himself. There are no other subscribers.

What is collected

The owner's mobile number, and the text of messages he sends to Rose's number (for example a video link he wants summarised).

How it is used

Only to answer the owner's own requests and send him alerts he asked for: a confirmation that a link was received, a link to the result, and notices that work needs his reply.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Message content is processed by the service providers needed to deliver and answer it (Twilio for delivery, Anthropic's Claude to read a link the owner sends) and is not sold or used for advertising.

Opting out

Reply STOP to stop all messages, HELP for help. Message frequency varies (a few per week). Message and data rates may apply.
