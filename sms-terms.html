Saad Paracha - Rose alerts: terms and conditions

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Program: Saad Paracha - Rose alerts, a personal assistant text program run by Saad Paracha (sole proprietor). The only recipient is the owner, Saad Paracha.

How to sign up: text START from your own mobile phone to the Rose alerts number. Details: sms-optin.html.

What you get: live NBA game and player score alerts on game days, alerts when your work needs your answer, and replies to video links you text in (a confirmation and a link to the result).

Message frequency varies: up to about 25 messages on a game day, fewer on other days.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: reply STOP at any time; you will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to rejoin.

Help: reply HELP, or open an issue at github.com/saadsenju.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
