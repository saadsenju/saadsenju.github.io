Rose text messages: terms and conditions

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Program: Rose personal assistant alerts, run by Saad Paracha (sole proprietor). The only recipient is the account owner, who opted in by entering his own mobile number in his own account settings and by texting Rose's number himself.

What you get: replies to links you text in (a confirmation and a link to the result) and alerts when your work needs your answer. Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per week.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: reply STOP at any time; you will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to rejoin.

Help: reply HELP, or see github.com/saadsenju.
