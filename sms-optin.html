Saad Paracha - Rose alerts: how to sign up

Rose alerts is a personal assistant text program run by Saad Paracha (sole proprietor). It sends messages to one person only: Saad Paracha, the owner. Nobody is added from a list, a bought list or a third party, and no one else can sign up.

How sign-up works

The owner signs up by texting the keyword START from his own mobile phone to the Rose alerts number +1 (267) 463-2903.

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Saad Paracha - Rose alerts: live NBA game and player score alerts on game days, alerts when your work needs your answer, and replies to video links you text in. Message frequency varies: up to about 25 messages on a game day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Terms · Privacy

Right after START, the owner gets this confirmation:
"Saad Paracha - Rose alerts: you're signed up for NBA score alerts, work alerts and link replies. Msg frequency varies (up to ~25/day on game days). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
