Sign up for Rose text messages

Rose is a personal assistant run by Saad Paracha (sole proprietor). It sends text alerts and replies to one person: the account owner. Nobody is added from a list, bought list or third party. There are two ways to sign up, and both need the owner's own action:

  1. Web form (below): the owner enters his own mobile number on this page and ticks the consent box. The box is not pre-ticked and is not required for anything else.
  2. Text keyword: the owner texts START (or any message) from his own phone to Rose's number. Rose replies: "Rose alerts: you're signed up for personal assistant alerts and replies. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Thanks. You'll get a confirmation text.

Sample messages: "NBA: James Harden: 30 PTS through 3 quarters" · "Got it. Your video steps are ready: [link]" · "Your Rose chat is waiting on your answer."
